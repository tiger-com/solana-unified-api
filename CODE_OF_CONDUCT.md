# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a straightforward, harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be direct about code and considerate about people. Reviewing a change harshly is fine;
  reviewing a person is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. A reasoned "no" is an answer, not an
  invitation to relitigate.
- Assume competence. Ask what someone was solving before assuming they got it wrong.

## What is unacceptable

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information,
sexualised language or imagery, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private security advisory or
direct contact. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and comments that violate this document, and may ban repeat offenders.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
